## Presentation boundary

- Keep the final presentation at or below 15 minutes.
- Demonstrate only the supporting artifacts that help explain the method and
  results.
- Show where the complete supporting package can be accessed.
- Do not attempt to present every request, response, source file, or rationale.
- Use the workflow figure, the candidate-level matrix, one or two focused
  case comparisons, the PATH-S06 extension, limitations, and conclusions.

The final presentation may point to the student evidence table and workbook,
but it must explain the important findings directly.
